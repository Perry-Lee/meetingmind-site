支持

录音、语音转文字和说话人分离全部在你的设备本地完成，不经过服务器。默认设置下，转录完成后原始音频即被删除。AI 纪要使用云端服务，需要你明确同意后才会发送转录文本。

常见问题

  • 没有网络还能用吗？可以。录音和转录完全离线可用，飞行模式下照常工作。只有 AI 纪要需要联网。
  • 录音会被上传吗？不会。音频始终留在本机，默认转录完就删除，也不会通过 iCloud 同步。使用 AI 功能时，只有转录的文字会在你同意后发送。
  • 怎么管理或取消订阅？在系统「设置 › 你的 Apple 账户 › 订阅」中管理。退款请通过 Apple 申请。
  • 换手机后数据会跟过去吗？开启 iCloud 同步后会通过你自己的 iCloud 账户同步。可在 app 设置中开关。
  • 怎么删除我的数据？删除对应会议即可一并删除录音、转录、摘要与任务；删除 app 会清除全部本地数据。

English

Support

Recording, transcription and speaker separation all run on your device, with no server involved. By default the original audio is deleted once transcription finishes. AI summaries use a cloud service and only send transcript text after you explicitly agree.

Frequently asked

  • Does it work without internet? Yes. Recording and transcription work fully offline, even in Airplane Mode. Only AI summaries need a connection.
  • Is my audio uploaded? No. Audio stays on the device, is deleted after transcription by default, and is never synced over iCloud. When you use an AI feature, only the transcript text is sent, and only after you consent.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? In Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device. Refunds are requested through Apple.
  • Will my data move to a new phone? Yes, if iCloud sync is enabled — it syncs through your own iCloud account. Toggle it in the app's settings.
  • How do I delete my data? Deleting a meeting removes its audio, transcript, summary and tasks. Deleting the app removes all local data.
