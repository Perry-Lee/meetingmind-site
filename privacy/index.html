03数据存放在哪里

你的设备

录音文件、转录文本、摘要、任务和所有会议信息都保存在本 app 的沙盒容器中，受 iOS 系统的文件保护机制保护。开发者无法访问这些数据。

关于原始音频：默认设置下，音频只在录制与转录期间存在，完成后即被删除。你可以在设置中开启「保留音频」以便日后回放；开启后音频文件仍留在本机，不会通过 iCloud 同步到其他设备，但如果你开启了 iOS 的整机 iCloud 备份，它会像其他 app 数据一样包含在该加密备份中。

你的 iCloud（可选）

本 app 使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 私有数据库，在你启用同步时把会议信息、转录文本、摘要和任务同步到你自己的 iCloud 账户，以便在你的设备之间保持一致。原始音频体积过大，被刻意排除在同步之外。数据由 Apple 加密存储，只有你能访问；开发者无权访问该数据库。你可以在 app 设置中关闭该同步，或在 iOS 系统设置中撤销本 app 的 iCloud 权限。此过程适用 Apple 隐私政策。

04云端 AI 处理（重要）

录音与转录在设备端完成，但 AI 摘要、AI 标题、任务提取和“问 AI” 由云端大模型服务生成。使用这些功能时会发生：

  • 会发送什么：该场会议的转录文本、你填写的会议标题与备注、以及输出语言设置。
  • 不会发送什么：音频文件本身、你的姓名或邮箱、设备标识符、通讯录、其他会议的内容。请求中不包含任何可用于识别你个人身份的账号信息。
  • 发送给谁：OpenRouter（OpenRouter, Inc.），它再将请求转发给所选的大模型提供方以返回结果。传输全程使用 HTTPS/TLS 加密。相关处理适用 OpenRouter 隐私政策，服务器可能位于你所在国家或地区之外。
  • 是否保存：开发者不在任何自有服务器上保存你的转录文本——本 app 没有服务器。返回的摘要只写回你的设备。

你的同意是前提。首次使用任一 AI 功能前，本 app 会明确告知并请求你的同意；在你同意之前，不会有任何转录文本离开设备。如果你不同意，录音、端侧转录、回放、手动记笔记和任务管理等功能依然可以完整使用。

如果你希望在同意之后停止一切云端处理，只需不再使用 AI 相关功能即可；删除并重新安装本 app 会将同意状态重置为未同意。

Meeting Notes (the “app”) is built and operated by Perry Lee, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app touches, where it is stored, when — if ever — it leaves your device, and how you stay in control of it.

01The core principle

Short version

The app has no server of its own. We do not create an account for you and we do not keep your meeting content on any server we control. Your data lives on your own device and, if you enable syncing, in your own iCloud account. The only thing ever sent to a third party is the transcript text you choose to send when you use an AI feature and have given explicit consent.

03Where your data lives

Your device

Audio files, transcripts, summaries, tasks and all meeting details are stored inside the app's sandbox container, protected by iOS file protection. The developer cannot access them.

About the original audio: by default it exists only while recording and transcribing, and is deleted afterwards. You can turn on “keep audio” in Settings to replay recordings later; kept audio stays on the device and is not synced to your other devices over iCloud, but if you have iOS device backup enabled it is included in that encrypted backup like any other app data.

Your iCloud (optional)

When syncing is enabled, the app uses Apple's CloudKit private database to sync meeting details, transcripts, summaries and tasks through your own iCloud account so they stay consistent across your devices. The original audio is deliberately excluded from that sync because of its size. Apple stores it encrypted and only you can access it; the developer has no access to that database. You can turn the sync off in the app's settings, or revoke iCloud access for the app in iOS Settings. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to this processing.

04Cloud AI processing (important)

Recording and transcription happen on device, but AI summaries, AI titles, task extraction and Ask AI are generated by a cloud large-language-model service. When you use those features:

  • What is sent: the transcript text of that meeting, the meeting title and notes you wrote, and your chosen output language.
  • What is not sent: the audio file itself, your name or email, device identifiers, your contacts, or the content of other meetings. The request carries no account information that identifies you personally.
  • Who receives it: OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc.), which routes the request to the selected model provider and returns the result. Transport is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. This processing is subject to the OpenRouter Privacy Policy, and servers may be located outside your country.
  • Retention: the developer keeps no copy of your transcript on any server — the app has none. The generated summary is written back only to your device.

Your consent comes first. Before the first use of any AI feature, the app explains this and asks for your consent. No transcript text leaves your device until you agree. If you decline, recording, on-device transcription, playback, manual notes and task management keep working in full.

If you have consented and later want all cloud processing to stop, simply stop using the AI features; deleting and reinstalling the app resets the consent state to “not granted”.

05System permissions

  • Microphone — to record meeting audio, only while you have actively started a recording.
  • Speech recognition — to use Apple's Speech framework, which converts speech to text on device.
  • Contacts — optional. Read only when you choose “add participant from Contacts”, and only the names you pick are stored. The app never reads or uploads your whole address book.
  • Reminders — optional. Used only when you tap “add to Reminders” to export tasks. The app does not read your existing reminders.

All of these can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security.
